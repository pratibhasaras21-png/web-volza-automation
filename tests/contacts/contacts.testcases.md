# Contact Parser — Test Cases

Source: QA backlog CSV. The Contact Parser section appears **twice** in the source spreadsheet:
- "Module10: Contact Parser" (rows ~697-706, first pass, 8 items) — brief, one line per case.
- "Module 10 : Contact Parser" (rows ~716-758, second pass, 12 items, numbered including a "6.1") — noticeably more detailed, with its own Preconditions/Steps/Expected-Result columns.

**Merge approach:** the second, more detailed pass is used as the primary source for every row below. Every item in the first pass maps onto an item already covered by the second pass (redirect-on-click, point deduction, grid data visible, find phone/email, manual contact request, manual contact request per-contact, report-incorrect) except for two that are strictly broader than their second-pass counterpart and are folded in rather than dropped:
- First-pass item 2 ("balance contact profile point deduction") — folded into TC-CONTACT-001, which now also covers the point-deduction check on redirect.
- First-pass item 4 ("all sections present in contact tab must be visible") — folded into TC-CONTACT-009, broadened from "Location/Branches/Social Media" alone to "all page sections."

Rows are deduped against the 10 actual automated test cases in `contact-parser.spec.js` — no 1:1 row per CSV line item, since several CSV steps (e.g. "go to Search tab", "select country and execute search") are setup/navigation for a case rather than a distinct case of their own.

**Preconditions (apply to the whole module):** Registered email already registered on app.volza.com; active paid plan (SME or Corporate); sufficient points/balance to request a manual contact.
