# My Download Tab — Test Cases

Source: QA backlog CSV, "Module 8: My Download tab" (rows ~659-675). Automated in `my-download.spec.js`.

**Preconditions (apply to the whole module):** SME/Corporate plan account, already authenticated. The CSV's granular checks (search-for-workspace option, name-filter option, sub-module fields — query ID/workspace name/shipment count/username, redownload, redownload across universal/global/advance search origins, workspace-name hyperlink redirect back to Search) are folded into the two broader end-to-end automated scenarios below rather than kept as separate rows, since no automated test exercises them in isolation.

| TC ID | Title | Priority | Preconditions | Steps | Expected Result | Spec Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC-DOWNLOAD-001 | Search + save workspace + filter + toolbar Download, verify query and Excel in My Downloads | P1 | SME/Corporate plan account | 1. Go to Search tab, select "All Country (Global Search)", enter product "oil", click Search. 2. Save the results as a uniquely-named workspace. 3. Apply a left FILTERS panel "Country of Origin" filter. 4. Click the toolbar Download button and confirm the chargeable-download modal. 5. Go to My Download tab (via the account icon's My Downloads sidebar link). 6. Verify the same saved workspace's query is listed (query ID, workspace name, shipment count, username). 7. Click the row's download link/icon and open the Excel file. | The download entry for the saved workspace query appears in My Downloads; clicking its download link/icon re-triggers the Excel download; the file opens successfully and contains real shipment data. | `my-download.spec.js` |
| TC-DOWNLOAD-002 | Direct toolbar Download without saving the workspace, verify it still appears in My Downloads | P1 | SME/Corporate plan account | 1. Go to Search tab, select a global country/database, enter product "oil", click Search — deliberately skip Save Workspace. 2. Click the toolbar Download button directly. 3. Go to My Download tab and confirm a new entry appeared. 4. Click the row's download link/icon and open the Excel file. | A new entry appears in My Downloads even though no workspace was explicitly saved, confirming Download doesn't require a prior Save Workspace step; the downloaded Excel opens successfully with real data, no error occurs. | `my-download.spec.js` |
